Requesting removal

Subtitles are user-submitted; this node works on notice and takedown. Include:

  • The track URL or id (/api/v1/subtitles/<id>) or enough detail to locate it (release fingerprint, language).
  • The work you hold rights to and your relationship to it.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the material is unlicensed.

If you would rather not mail, every release page has a removal-request form under each subtitle; it needs no account and reaches the moderators directly.

What happens

  • A valid request withdraws the track (or the whole release): it stops appearing in lookups and downloads, and dumps made afterwards exclude it.
  • Withdrawal is a soft delete: the record stays, so the removal can be explained later and reversed if a request turns out to be invalid.
  • Accounts that repeatedly infringe are disabled.
  • Nothing is hard-deleted, and a dump or mirror taken before a withdrawal still holds the content until its own operator acts on it.
